# 🚀 Anas Technology: PropTech Business Development Playbook

This document is the official Business Development strategy for selling our **Custom PropTech & Deal Sourcing CRM** to the UK Real Estate Market.

---

## 1. What Are We Selling?
We are selling **High-Ticket Custom Software / ERPs** to UK Property Sourcers.
We DO NOT sell a £50/month SaaS subscription. We act as a "PropTech Engineering Agency". 

**The Product (Custom Property Sourcing CRM):**
* **1-Click Deal Packaging:** Auto-generates branded PDF deal packs (No more Canva).
* **Live Property Data:** Integrates with Rightmove, Land Registry, and EPC APIs.
* **Auto-Calculators:** Calculates BRRR, Gross Yield, and ROI instantly (No more Excel).
* **Private Investor Portal:** A custom portal for their investors on their own domain.

**The Price:** 
* **Setup Fee:** £2,000 - £4,000 (Upfront)
* **Monthly Retainer:** £100 - £200/month (For hosting, API usage, and maintenance)

---

## 2. Who is our Target Audience? (ICP)

✅ **TARGET THESE COMPANIES (The Buyers):**
* Property Sourcing Agencies
* Deal Packaging Companies
* Property Investment Firms
* Property Wealth Management
* Companies mentioning "BRRR", "BTL (Buy-to-Let)", or "HMO" in their bio.

❌ **DO NOT TARGET (Waste of Time):**
* High Street Estate Agents (e.g., Dexters, Foxtons, local brokers selling homes to families). They do not care about ROI or Deal Packs.

**Decision Makers (Who to message):**
* Founder / Co-Founder
* Managing Director (MD)
* Director of Property Sourcing
* Head of Investments

---

## 3. How to Find Them on LinkedIn

Use the following Boolean search string in LinkedIn Sales Navigator or standard search (Set Location to **United Kingdom**):

`("Property Sourcing" OR "Deal Packaging" OR "Property Investment") AND ("Founder" OR "Director" OR "Managing Director")`
